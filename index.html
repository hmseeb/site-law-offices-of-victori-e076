Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

What is the difference between a PC 273.5 and a PC 243(e)(1) charge?

California Penal Code 273.5 is the charge for corporal injury to a spouse or cohabitant and can be filed as a felony or a misdemeanor. Penal Code 243(e)(1) is domestic battery — often described as the lesser of the two, but still a serious charge with real consequences. How your case is charged shapes the defense strategy we build for you.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. The Law Offices of Victoria Clemans offers free, confidential initial consultations. Call (310) 488-6357 or submit the form on this page and we will respond promptly.

What should I do if I have been served with a restraining order?

Do not ignore it. A restraining order can affect where you live, your access to your home, and your firearm rights, and it carries long-term consequences. Contact our office immediately so we can review the paperwork and prepare for your hearing.

Which areas do you serve?

We defend clients throughout Los Angeles County, including Los Angeles, Santa Monica, and surrounding communities, with offices in Santa Monica and Los Angeles.

What does a Title IX case involve?

Title IX governs how schools respond to allegations of sexual misconduct or harassment. These are campus disciplinary proceedings — separate from criminal court — and the outcome can affect your enrollment and future. We represent students and faculty throughout the investigation and hearing process.

How quickly should I contact a lawyer?

As soon as possible. Early involvement allows us to gather evidence, speak with witnesses, and intervene before charges or decisions become harder to change. Contact us the moment you learn you are under investigation.
